BEng Project: Subsea Actuator for Sensing Applications in Energetic Tidal Channels.

Overview

Designed and developed a subsea actuator for the University of Edinburgh’s Institute for Energy Systems, enabling precise positioning of acoustic Doppler velocimeter (ADV) clusters for 3D flow field reconstruction upstream of tidal turbines.

The actuator forms part of a high-torque, low-power pan-and-tilt gimbal engineered for long-duration (~1 year) deployments in harsh tidal environments.

Design Inputs:
  • Flow velocity data from Doppler measurements
  • Tight backlash tolerance requirements
  • Limited onboard battery capacity
  • £10,000 total system budget
Design Outputs:
  • Designed actuator from first principles
  • Produced full mechanical drawings and bill of materials (BOM)
  • Conducted FEM analysis and structural validation
  • Performed shaft, bearing strength and corrosion calculations
  • Developed sealing and anti-fouling strategy
  • Defined maintenance and post-deployment refurbishment plan
Reference:
Harding, S., Dorward, M., Sellar, B., and Richmond, M. (2021). Field validation of an actuated convergent-beam acoustic Doppler profiler for high resolution flow mapping. Measurement Science and Technology, 32(4), 045904.
